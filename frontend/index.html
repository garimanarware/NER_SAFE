⛰️ North Eastern Region Vulnerability Context

The North Eastern Region (NER) of India comprises eight states characterized by active tectonics, intense seasonal monsoon precipitation, young fragile sedimentary geology, and deeply dissected relief. As a consequence, road networks such as NH-27 (Assam-Meghalaya), NH-29 (Nagaland-Manipur), and NH-10 (Sikkim) experience recurrent slope destabilization, disrupting essential supply routes and endangering lives.

NER-SAFE is an AI-assisted multi-signal disaster intelligence platform created to bridge the operational gap between macroscopic satellite surveillance and microscopic site-specific geotechnical monitoring. By synthesizing elevation topography (Copernicus DEM 90m), dynamic precipitation (Open-Meteo), geological history (10,773 landslide inventory entries), and edge sensor telemetry, the platform empowers district disaster administrations with explainable early warnings.
